Add agent lifecycle events for asynchronous and long-running executions:
`gen_ai.agent.paused`, `gen_ai.agent.checkpointed`, `gen_ai.agent.resumed`,
and `gen_ai.agent.pause.resolved` (resolution: `approved` / `refused` /
`expired`), with `gen_ai.agent.execution.id` correlating execution segments
across suspend/resume boundaries and the flat
`gen_ai.agent.resumed_from.type` / `.id` pair identifying the suspended
boundary a resumed segment continues from.
